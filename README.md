# KarmixTech_BankingLoanRiskDashboard

## 📊 Banking & Loan Risk Dashboard | Power BI

### Business Problem
Banks and lending institutions need continuous visibility into their loan portfolio to control risk, track profitability, and ensure repayment discipline. This dashboard was built to help stakeholders answer three core questions:
- **What happened?** — Current state of the loan portfolio (funded amount, payments, interest rates, term mix)
- **Why it happened?** — Which segments (grade, purpose, home ownership, state, loan status) are driving risk or performance
- **What action should be taken?** — Where to tighten underwriting, follow up on repayments, or reallocate lending focus

### Overview
An interactive Power BI dashboard analyzing loan portfolio performance, credit risk, customer profile, and repayment behavior. Built as part of the **Karmix Tech Data Analyst Internship**, following an industry-level approach focused on business storytelling rather than just visualization.

### Key KPIs
- Total Funded Amount
- Reported Payment
- Average Interest Rate
- Payment Ratio
- Portfolio Risk Indicator

### Dashboard Features
- **Trend Analysis:** Loan amount trend over time, loan distribution by month
- **Risk Segmentation:** Loan distribution by credit grade, loan purpose, and home ownership vs. interest rate
- **Portfolio Mix:** Loan term mix (short-term vs. long-term)
- **Filters/Slicers:** State, Loan Status, Paid Status, Purpose — for drill-down analysis

### Key Insights
- Portfolio risk is concentrated in specific loan grades and purposes, visible through the risk indicator and grade-wise breakdown
- Repayment performance (Payment Ratio) varies by loan status and term, highlighting where collections follow-up should focus
- Interest rate trends across home ownership categories point to pricing/risk segments worth reviewing

### Recommendations
- Prioritize monitoring of loan grades/purposes showing higher risk concentration
- Strengthen underwriting checks for segments with lower payment ratios
- Use state-level and purpose-level filters to guide targeted portfolio strategy

Power BI (Data Modeling, DAX Measures, Interactive Visuals)
